# SMART CAMPUS 360 — TEACHER USER MANUAL

**Audience:** Faculty (role: `teacher`).
**Sign in:** `teacher@gechassan.ac.in` / `Teacher@123` (demo — this account is already
verified for priority).
A teacher's priority benefits apply **only after Main Admin verification** — see §4.

---

## 1. What a teacher can do
Everything a student can (navigation, lost & found, canteen orders) **plus**:
* **Automatic queue priority** at the canteen — verified teachers only.
* A **priority badge** on the profile page confirming verification status.

## 2. Campus Navigation
Identical to the student manual: search locations, compute A\*/Dijkstra routes, and use
the Street-View photo walkthrough. Useful for guiding parents, guests and new students.

## 3. Lost & Found
* Report lost or found items with photos; the AI ranks candidates and notifies you.
* Claim found items with a private proof statement (same governed workflow as students).
* Your matches list is visible only to you and the Main Admin.

## 4. Getting verified (required for canteen priority)
1. Register / log in as **Teacher** with your department, employee ID and designation.
2. Until a Main Admin verifies you, your profile shows *Priority: not active* and your
   canteen orders queue as normal orders.
3. After verification you get a notification; your profile then shows the
   **Verified Teacher** badge and every future order is treated as priority.
   (Verification can also be revoked by the admin — e.g. on leaving staff.)

## 5. Smart Canteen — teacher-first booking
### How priority works for you
* **At booking (slot capacity):** a share of every pickup slot's capacity (25% by default)
  is **reserved for verified teachers** until the slot begins — so even a "full" student
  slot may still accept your order. Reservations release at slot start; students are never
  locked out indefinitely.
* **At the kitchen:** the Canteen Admin's live queue is sorted **priority-first**, so your
  confirmed orders move to *Preparing* before regular orders of the same slot.
* Your order pages show a **PRIORITY** badge and an earlier **estimated ready time**.
### Booking steps
1. **Canteen** → add items to the tray → **Cart**.
2. Choose a pickup slot (≥ 15 min lead time) → **Checkout** → simulated payment.
3. Receive your **QR pickup token** + estimated ready time; watch status transitions in
   **My Orders** (notifications arrive at each change).
4. Collect at the counter — the admin verifies your QR; order closes as *Collected*.
### Cancellations & complaints
Cancel before preparation for the automatic sandbox refund, or raise a refund complaint
from **My Orders** if an item was wrong or never served — the canteen team authorises and
processes full or partial refunds with a visible trail.
> All payments in this build are **sandbox-simulated**; no real money is processed.

## 6. Privacy notes
* Your phone number is masked from visitors and other users.
* The kitchen queue shows your name and priority status to canteen staff only.
